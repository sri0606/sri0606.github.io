Best Emerging Technology Award

In my first hackathon experience at SpartaHack, our team won the Best Emerging Technology Award for our project, BrainWaive. BrainWaive is a cross-platform application leveraging AI to craft personalized study guides for students through the generation of flashcards and quizzes. We developed BrainWaive as a robust personalized education web application, harnessing the capabilities of OpenAI’s GPT-3 model. It offered impactful learning experiences, featuring custom quizzes, flashcards, and career resources tailored to the educational needs of students.
